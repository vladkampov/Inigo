## Invocation policy and spelling scoring

- Undo now passes the current invocation policy to guarded replacement. Readback
  stops if the source becomes excluded during an operation, and the temporary
  style-retention check also respects current exclusions.
- Two regressions verify that excluded Undo performs no text reads/writes and
  policy revocation after a write stops further readback/caret writes.
- Spelling error ranges are sorted once and traversed with a forward cursor.
  Tests cover unsorted/overlapping ranges, UTF-16 prefix offsets, empty evidence,
  and 5,000 misspelled words in a 19,999-unit draft.
- All 13 targeted editor/scoring tests passed. The local debug scoring stress run
  averaged roughly 4 ms over ten samples; there is no hard timing gate or claim
  about other devices or complete request latency.
